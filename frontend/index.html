Target Audience

Who is it for?

Designed specifically to bridge educational inequalities for secondary school communities.

Primary Users

Students

Secondary school learners preparing for national exams (Senior 4, Senior 5, Senior 6) on phones and limited mobile data.

  • Browse learning materials: Find curriculum-aligned notes organized by subject and level in one place.
  • Take practice quizzes: Test your understanding with multiple-choice questions before real exams.
  • See your marks: Get instant scores, detailed feedback, and track your history over time.
Join as a Student →
Educators

Teachers

Subject teachers who want a simple, affordable tool to support all of their students beyond the classroom walls.

  • Create courses: Organize subjects and levels (e.g. Mathematics – S6) with no complex setup.
  • Post materials: Publish revision notes and useful links once to reach every student in the course.
  • Build quizzes: Create multiple-choice quizzes with options and automatic grading keys.
  • Track student results: Review class performance analytics to see where students need extra help.

Core Features

Built for Low-Data Learning

High-impact features designed to deliver maximum learning outcomes with minimum mobile data usage.

Courses by Subject & Level

Browse curriculum-aligned study materials grouped neatly by subject and grade (Senior 4, 5, 6). No more losing files buried in crowded WhatsApp chats.

Quizzes with Instant Scores

Practice with multiple-choice quizzes, submit your answers, and receive your score instantly with explanations to find weak areas before real national exams.

Works on Phones & Slow Networks

No heavy app to install. Built with clean HTML, Bootstrap 5, and vanilla JavaScript that loads quickly even on basic smartphones and 2G/3G connections.
